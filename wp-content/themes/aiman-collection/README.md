# 👑 Aiman Collection — WordPress & WooCommerce Theme

**Ultra-Luxury Dawoodi Bohra Haute Couture, Ridas & Pret WordPress Theme**

## Features
- **WooCommerce Ready**: Full support for product catalogs, cart, checkout, and single product views.
- **1-Click WhatsApp Order Builder**: Direct order placement with pre-formatted product specifications and pricing.
- **Bilingual & Multi-Currency Engine**: English & Urdu (`Noto Nastaliq Urdu`) with live currency converter (PKR, USD, AED, GBP, SAR).
- **Live AJAX Search & Autocomplete**: Instant search with product thumbnail, category, and formatted price.
- **Sliding Mini-Cart Drawer**: Seamless AJAX cart updates.
- **Quick View Modals**: Fast product preview and custom specs without leaving the page.
- **Theme Customizer**: Live controls for WhatsApp number, announcement ticker, hero banner, and bank details.
- **Demo Importer**: 1-click sample Dawoodi Bohra catalog importer.
